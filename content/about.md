## My story

<!-- Replace this section with your own story: why you chose this field, what keeps you curious, where you are heading. -->

My path into drug research started with a basic question in molecular biology: how do cells sense stress and decide what to do about it? Answering it during my graduate training taught me to connect molecular mechanisms to observable outcomes.

Today I apply the same mindset to drug development — asking not only *whether* a candidate works, but *why*. I write here to keep learning in public and to share what I find with others in the field.
