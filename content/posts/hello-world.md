Antibody therapeutics have moved far beyond the classic monoclonal IgG. Bispecifics, antibody–drug conjugates, and brain-penetrant formats now make up a large share of clinical pipelines, and AI is starting to change how these molecules are designed.

I'm new to antibody therapeutics. Recently, though, I had the opportunity to attend a lecture on AI-based antibody design, and I found it fascinating. It made me realize that, with the help of AI, I can teach myself this field step by step.

This blog is where I'll keep my study notes along the way. I hope to build on what I learn here and use it to grow my career.
